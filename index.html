🧻 How to Play

  • 🎲 Roll the die to move toward the castle.
  • 💩 Land on poop? Mash W or tap WIPE before the timer runs out.
  • ✅ Wipe in time for points. Miss it and you're knocked back.
  • 🔥 Chain wipes for a streak bonus.
  • ⚡ Lucky tiles grant a shield that blocks one bush catch.
  • 🌳 Angry bushes chase you — get caught and you lose points and ground. A third bush joins late!
  • ⚔️ Press L to call in Legion for a protective shield, or R to send Reaper to clean a poop zone ahead. Once each per turn.
  • 🏰 Reach the castle to win.

🔒 Privacy Policy

You can play as a guest with nothing stored beyond your own device, or create an account to appear on the global leaderboard. Here's exactly what each involves.

Playing as a guest (no account): everything stays in your browser's local storage, on your own device — your best score and top-5 local runs, whether you've seen the "How to Play" intro, and your sound/music preferences. Nothing is sent anywhere. Clearing your browser data removes all of it.

If you create an account: we store your username, a securely hashed password (never the password itself, never in plain text), and the scores you submit, in a database, solely to power the global leaderboard. No email address is required or collected.

There's no analytics, no third-party tracking, and nothing is sold or shared. You can delete your account and all associated scores at any time from the account menu.

❓ FAQ

How do I play?
Roll the die to move toward the castle. Land on 💩 and mash W or tap WIPE before the timer runs out. Watch out for the angry bushes chasing you.

Is my score saved?
Your best score and top 5 runs are always saved locally in your browser, no account needed. Sign in (👤) to also submit scores to the global leaderboard (🏆).

Do I need an account?
No — guest play works fully offline-local. An account is only needed to appear on the shared global leaderboard.

Can I turn off the sound or music?
Yes, use the 🔊 and 🎵 buttons above the board. Your preference is remembered.

What do the ⚡ tiles do?
They grant a one-time shield that blocks the next bush catch.

Does it work on mobile?
Yes, tap to roll and tap WIPE — no keyboard required.
